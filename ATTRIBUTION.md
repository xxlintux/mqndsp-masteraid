# Attribution & Acknowledgments

Masteraid was built by standing on the shoulders of giants in the open-source audio development community.

---

## 1. Chris Johnson (Airwindows)

The acoustic soul, analog saturation curves, slew damping, tape dynamics, and capacitor filter topologies in Masteraid are powered by the open-source DSP research of **Chris Johnson ([Airwindows](https://www.airwindows.com/))**.

Chris's code is a masterclass in pro-audio programming:
- **Lean**: No superfluous abstractions, no bloat, and no unnecessary CPU cycles.
- **Elegant**: Pure, pristine mathematical formulas that model the physical behavior of analog hardware with laser focus.
- **Deeply Musical**: Transfer curves and time-constants tuned by ear and proven across thousands of records.

Without Chris's decades of tireless dedication and radical generosity in releasing his work under the MIT license, Masteraid simply would not exist.

**If Masteraid brings value to your music, please support Chris directly:**
👉 **[Support Airwindows on Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/airwindows)**

## 2. Robbert van der Helm (`nih-plug`)

The plugin architecture, audio buffer abstraction, CLAP and VST3 host bindings, parameter automation systems, and GUI bridge are powered by **[`nih-plug`](https://github.com/robbert-vdh/nih-plug)**, created by Robbert van der Helm.

`nih-plug` represents the state of the art in modern, memory-safe, zero-overhead Rust audio plugin development.

---

## 3. Vizia & Femtovg

Masteraid's GPU-accelerated immediate/retained tactile user interface is rendered using **[`vizia`](https://github.com/vizia/vizia)** and Femtovg, enabling 60fps hardware-accelerated visuals without bloated webview runtimes.
